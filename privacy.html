Privacy Policy

Effective August 23, 2026 · How S333XHUB handles your data

What we collect

When you sign up: your email address and the display name you choose. When you use the app: the messages you send, your notification preferences, your block list, any reports you file, and a record of posts you've unlocked. If you allow notifications on the real app: a device push token. That's it — no contacts, no location, no tracking across other apps.

How we use it

To run the app: showing your name on your messages, delivering chat, remembering your unlocks, sending only the notifications you've left enabled, and handling reports and blocks. We don't sell your data, and there is no advertising in the app.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (our database and file-storage provider) on servers in the United States. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google and our payment partner RevenueCat — we never see your card details. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security commitments.

Who can see what

Your display name and messages are visible to other members in the chats you're in. Your email is never shown to other users. The Artist can see reported content in order to moderate. We share data with no one else, except if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Deleted messages are hidden immediately and purged in routine cleanups. When you delete your account, your profile, messages, preferences, and purchase records are permanently removed.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. Without the app: email southeasterngroup28@gmail.com from your account's email address, or use our account-deletion web page, and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Children

S333XHUB is for users 17 and older and is not directed at children. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 17 we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll announce it in the app. Questions or requests about your data: southeasterngroup28@gmail.com.
