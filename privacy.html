Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

Who we are

This policy is issued by RONSO LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we"), which operates S333XHUB for the artist releasing as Mazze and S333XGOD ("the Artist").

What we collect

Account: your email address, your password, the display name you choose, and a profile photo and a background photo if you add them. Using the app: messages, voice notes and photos you send in chat, direct messages with the Artist or with other members, comments, reactions and poll votes, fan mail you send and the photo, video or audio attached to it, your notification settings, your block list, reports you file, and a record of posts you've unlocked. Tickets and pieces: your display name at the time of purchase, your shipping address for a piece, what you bought and its edition number, and Stripe's reference for the payment. We never receive your card number. Notifications: a device push token if you turn them on. Moderation: whether your account has been suspended. Crash reports: if the app crashes, we record the error, the app version and your device platform, with your account id, so we can fix it. We do not collect your contacts or your location, and we do not track you across other apps.

How we use it

To run the app: showing your name on your messages, delivering chat, remembering your unlocks, getting your ticket scanned and your piece shipped, sending only the notifications you've left enabled, and handling reports and blocks. We don't sell your data, and there is no advertising in the app.

Where it lives

Accounts, messages, photos, orders and tickets are stored with Supabase on servers in the United States. Your password is stored in a form we cannot read. Post unlocks go through Apple's in-app purchase system; RevenueCat keeps the receipt record so unlocks can be restored. Shop orders and show tickets are paid through Stripe, which handles your card details and receives your name and email address for its customer record. We never see your card number. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's notification service. Fan mail reaches the Artist's inbox through Resend. Each of these companies handles data only to provide its part of the service, not for its own use.

Who can see what

Other fans see your display name, your profile photo if you set one, and the messages, voice notes, photos and comments you post in the chats and threads they are in. If you opened the community chat in the last 30 days, other members of it can also see your name and photo in its member list, unless one of you has blocked the other. If you buy a piece, your display name and edition number appear in that drop's registry, which every member can see. The Artist sees what fans send the Artist: the community chat, direct messages with the Artist, fan mail, reports, and the name and address on any order, so it can be shipped. A direct message between two fans is visible to the two people in it, and to the safety check described below. In a direct message the other person can see when you last opened it, and you can see the same. Your email address is never shown to other fans. For a physical order we give your name and address to the shipping carrier. Beyond that we share nothing, unless the law requires it.

Safety scanning

Messages you send in the app, including direct messages, are checked automatically for signs of child exploitation, scams, drug dealing, and similar harm. Most messages are read only by software. When a check flags a conversation, the flagged messages and a few messages around them may be reviewed by RONSO LLC and the Artist, and the account may be suspended. A copy of the flagged conversation is kept as safety evidence, together with account records for the accounts involved, such as the email address, sign-in IP addresses and times, device details, and purchase references. That evidence can remain after the accounts involved are deleted, where safety or the law requires it, and is shared only with the authorities. The automated check runs through Anthropic, an AI company, which processes the text only to perform the check. Where the law requires it, unlawful material is reported to the authorities, including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. A message or comment the Artist removes is hidden from the app but kept for moderation. When you delete your account in the app, your profile, profile photo, background photo, messages, comments, votes, notification preferences, block list, reports and unlock history are erased immediately. Photos and voice notes you sent in chat and the files attached to your fan mail are removed within 30 days. If you ask by email, everything is removed within 30 days. Four things stay: shop orders and show tickets (your name, shipping address, what you bought and what you paid) are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, up to seven years, with your account no longer attached to them; fan mail already sent to the Artist stays in the Artist's inbox; crash reports are kept without your account id; and conversations the safety check flagged are kept as safety evidence, as the Safety scanning section describes.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. Confirm once and the account is gone. Without the app, email support@s333xhub.com from your account's email address, or use the account-deletion page on our site, and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Children

S333XHUB is for users 17 and older and is not directed at children. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 17 we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll announce it in the app. Questions or requests about your data: support@s333xhub.com, or by mail to RONSO LLC, 10437 NW 82nd St, Unit 12, Miami, FL 33178.
